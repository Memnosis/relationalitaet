## 1. Prologue: The Bridge of Transience

We all feel the passage of time as an unstoppable flow. In classical physics, this flow is often perceived as a fourth dimension—a rigid container in which events unfold. But what if time is not a container at all? What if it is merely the "heartbeat" of a giant computational process?

In this paper, we guide the reader from the familiar notion of a linear time axis toward an understanding of time as the **causal update rate**. We demonstrate that information (Relational Information) is not just the description of the world, but its actual building material. In this model, time is the rhythm in which the ontological code (Rel1) is executed on the hardware of the vacuum (Rel0). This shift in perspective not only resolves philosophical paradoxes but also provides the mathematical foundation for the unification of information and energy.

## 2. Ontological Foundation: The Trinity of Temporality

The temporality of reality can be precisely explained via the Trinity structure (Vacuum Potential, Manifest Structure, Processual Loop).

### 2.1 Rel0 (Vacuum Potential): The Timelessness of Silence
At the level of pure potential (Rel0), time does not exist. We define Rel0 as the phase space of all theoretically possible states—a state of infinite dimensionality that requires no clocking. Drawing on Martin Heidegger’s (1927) analysis of temporality, we understand Rel0 as the "abyss" (Ab-Grund) from which temporal extension must first emerge. It is the absolute baseline of silence.

### 2.2 Rel1 (Manifest Structure): The Solidification of Form
The manifested structure (Rel1) is the result of an informative choice. When a relation "snaps in" from the noise of Rel0, an identity is created. This identity is static in itself—a "frozen moment." In traditional ontology (Parmenides, ~500 B.C.E.), this is the truly being. However, a world consisting only of Rel1 would be dead; it would be a still image without motion.

### 2.3 The Loop (Processual Rhythm): The Birth of Time
It is only through the **Relational Loop** (the processual execution of unity) that what we perceive as time arises. Time is the recursive comparison between the current state (input) and the next state (output). We formally define time as the **frequency of informative bit-flips** in the relational grid. As Norbert Wiener (1948) recognized in cybernetics, control and temporality are inextricably linked with feedback loops. The Loop is the heartbeat of existence.

## 3. The Derivation of Time from Information

We radicalize John Wheeler’s (1990) "It from Bit" doctrine. If the world consists fundamentally of bits (distinctions), then the passage of time must be the process in which these bits are updated.

### 3.1 Speed of Light as a Hardware Limit
In *Relational Mathematics*, we do not derive the speed of light $c$ as a travel speed, but as the maximum **causal update rate**. Each grid segment of space can only change its state with a finite frequency (Planck rhythm). $c$ is thus the upper limit of data transfer per clock cycle. Time dilation, as described by Albert Einstein (1915), is in our model the local slowing of this update rate caused by a high information density (mass).

### 3.2 The Thermodynamic Price (Landauer Limit)
The passage of time requires energy. Rolf Landauer (1961) proved that erasing information inevitably generates heat. In *New Relationality*, we demonstrate that every reset cycle at a grid node represents an additional energetic expenditure. Time "costs" the universe energy, as it involves the constant rewriting of the Rel1 structure on the Rel0 baseline. This provides the physical grounding for the entropic arrow of time (Prigogine & Stengers, 1984).

## 4. Mathematics of Time: The Calculus of Clock Frequency

To move the understanding of time from metaphor to measurable quantity, we define the mathematical clocking of the relational grid. In *New Relationality*, time is not a continuum but the discrete frequency of state updates.

### 4.1 The Fundamental Clock Frequency ($f_{rel}$)
We postulate that every physical interaction requires at least one clock cycle of the grid. The maximum clocking $f_{max}$ is limited by the Planck time $t_P$.

$$
f_{max} = \frac{1}{t_P} \approx 1.85 \times 10^{43} \text{ Hz}
$$

Any observed duration $T$ is thus the sum of discrete clock cycles $N$, where $N \in \mathbb{N}$:
$$
T = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \Delta t_i \quad \text{where} \quad \Delta t_i \geq t_P
$$

### 4.2 Emergence of Causal Velocity ($c$)
The speed of light $c$ is not an arbitrary constant of nature, but the structural conductivity of the vacuum. In a grid with mesh size $l_P$, information can propagate exactly one grid segment per clock cycle $t_P$.

$$
v_{max} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{l_P}{t_P} = c
$$

### 4.4 The Paradox of the First Tick (Emergence Trigger)
One of the most critical questions in the 100-Point Audit concerns the transition from ontic silence (Rel0) to temporal manifestation (Loop). If Rel0 is timeless, how can a "beginning" be defined? We resolve this through the **stochastic fluctuation of relation**. In a field of infinite potentials, the probability of a "null relation" over eternal timeframes ($\infty$) is zero. The first tick is not a temporal event *within* time, but the **symmetry breaking of silence**, induced by the necessary self-encounter of the code (Relational Primordial Collapse).

### 4.5 The Energetic Signature of the Reset
We deepen the Landauer Limit: every clock tick in the universe corresponds to an information erasure (reset of the previous state). Since every reset requires $\Delta E \geq k_B T \ln 2$ of energy, the "expansion" of the universe is simultaneously an energetic redistribution. Cosmic background radiation is thus not just a remnant of the Big Bang, but the cumulative "waste heat noise" of the ongoing time update (Loop dissipation).

It follows that causality is bound to the clocking of the medium. Massless information (photons) utilize the maximum update rate of the grid.

### 4.3 The Relational Refraction Index ($\mathcal{N}_{rel}$)
The presence of information (mass/energy) increases the local grid density $\Omega_{rel}$. This leads to an "update delay," which we perceive as time dilation. We introduce the **Refraction Index of Time**:

$$
\mathcal{N}_{rel} = 1 + \Omega_{rel} = \frac{f_{global}}{f_{local}}
$$

Where $f_{global}$ describes the undisturbed baseline clocking (Rel0) and $f_{local}$ describes the clocking slowed down by Rel1 structures.
